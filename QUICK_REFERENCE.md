# Service Quick Sell - Quick Reference Card

## 🎯 What Is It?
A fast checkout mode for services in the POS system. Click a service card to instantly add it to cart.

---

## 🚀 How to Use

### Quick Sell (Default Mode)
1. Go to **POS → Services**
2. Ensure **"Quick Sell"** is selected
3. Click any service card
4. Service added to cart instantly! ✓
5. Proceed to checkout

### Queue Mode (Detailed Orders)
1. Go to **POS → Services**
2. Click **"Queue"** button
3. Click any service card
4. Fill in customer details
5. Create order

## ⚡ Quick Sell vs Queue

| Feature | Quick Sell | Queue |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Speed | ⚡ Instant | 🐢 Slower |
| Steps | 2 clicks | 10+ clicks |
| Forms | None | Multiple |
| Best for | Walk-ins | Appointments |
| Customer info | Auto-filled | Manual entry |
| Custom fields | Skipped | Available |
| Scheduling | No | Yes |

---

## 💡 When to Use Each Mode

### Use Quick Sell For:
- ✅ Walk-in customers
- ✅ Standard pricing
- ✅ Quick transactions
- ✅ High-volume sales
- ✅ Simple services

### Use Queue For:
- ✅ Scheduled appointments
- ✅ Custom pricing needed
- ✅ Special requirements
- ✅ Detailed tracking needed
- ✅ Multiple custom fields

## ⚠️ Important Notes

1. **Default Mode**: Quick Sell is the default for fastest service
2. **Cart Integration**: Works with products and other services
3. **Payment Methods**: All payment methods available (Cash, Kopesha, etc.)
4. **Service Orders**: Still creates service orders for tracking
5. **Permissions**: Respects user access permissions

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
