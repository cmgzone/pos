Under the Hood

The Hybrid Synchronization Engine

Learn how our local-first state stays in 100% transactional consistency with our serverless Postgres databases in the cloud.

1. Speed & Offline Uptime

The Flutter application records sales, shifts, and inventory directly into a local SQLite database inside the device. Transactions execute instantly with zero network round trips.

2. Background Syncer

A Node.js & Express API acts as the bridge. A background thread polls for changes and transmits encrypted transaction payloads using a safe sequential cursor tracking mechanism.

3. Neon Postgres Storage

Cloud records are safely committed to Neon PostgreSQL. Built-in indexes and horizontal scaling support multi-store real-time analytics, instant stock alerts, and global operations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about Piki POS architecture, offline caching, and data migrations? Read on.

How does local-first database cache state without conflicts?

Piki POS implements localized UUID generators instead of serial incremental keys. When a sale is processed offline, it is written as a secure local transaction row in SQLite with a sync status flagged as 'pending'. When the client transitions online, a secure incremental cursor sends batches to the Express Node.js API, where updates are reconciled based on the latest updated_at server timestamp.

What happens to the physical cash drawer balances during a shift?

Our payment system uses flag-based sorting: Cash sales are tagged with is_cash_drawer = 1, while credit sales (Kopesha) and online banking (M-Pesa, card) are tagged with is_cash_drawer = 0. During shift handovers, the cash register reports exactly the expected physical cash drawer volume separate from online/ledger transactions, minimizing reconciliation overhead.

Is Neon Serverless Postgres secure and durable?

Yes. Neon is an enterprise cloud serverless PostgreSQL architecture. The database splits storage and compute. Your store transactions are safely replicated across redundant storage nodes, guaranteeing instant failover protection, automatic cloud backups, and seamless scalability to handle millions of invoices.

Can we define custom digital payment methods?

Absolutely. The settings panel in Piki POS provides full administration over payment configurations. You can deactivate cash features, activate local credit methods, or integrate custom digital gateways. New payment configurations automatically sync across your sales registers instantly.
