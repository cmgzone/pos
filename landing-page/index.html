06—Questions

Things people ask before they install.

Does it really work when the internet is down?

Yes. Checkout runs on the device, so cashiers keep selling when the connection drops. When it comes back, pending sales sync to the cloud. No sale is lost to a dead router.

Can it handle both products and services?

Yes. Piki POS supports product inventory, variants, categories, and purchases alongside service orders, car wash queues, and salons. You can ring up products and services in the same sale.

What about payments and KRA eTIMs?

Cash, Kopesha credit, and configurable payment methods are built in. M-Pesa checkout and KRA eTIMs submission work once you add the relevant credentials. Receipts print or send by message.

Can I import existing sales and stock from Excel?

Yes. Import products, sales, customers, and expenses from .xlsx or .csv. Piki AI maps messy headers, skips note rows, and shows you a preview before anything is applied.

Is my data safe if a device is lost or broken?

Sales are saved on the device first, then backed up to the cloud with encryption. Approved devices can recover records, so a lost or broken tablet doesn't take your business history with it.
