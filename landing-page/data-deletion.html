Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: June 18, 2026

You can request deletion of Piki POS account data, support request data, online storefront order data, and messaging connection data by contacting Piki POS support.

How to Request Deletion

Email support@pikipos.com with the subject line "Data deletion request". Include the business name, account email, phone number, and a short description of the data you want deleted.

Verification

To protect business records, we may ask you to verify that you own or administer the business account before deleting account or cloud sync data.

What Can Be Deleted

Depending on your request and legal requirements, we can delete or anonymize account profile data, cloud sync records, online storefront order records, support messages, and messaging connection metadata controlled by Piki POS.

What May Be Retained

Some records may be retained when needed for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, accounting, tax, legal compliance, backup recovery windows, or legitimate business operations.

Timing

We aim to review deletion requests within a reasonable time after verification. If deletion cannot be completed immediately, we will explain the reason where appropriate.

Contact

For deletion requests or privacy questions, contact support@pikipos.com.
