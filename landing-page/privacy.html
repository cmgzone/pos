Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

Piki POS provides point-of-sale software, cloud sync, online storefronts, customer order tools, messaging integrations, payments support, reporting, and business support services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use Piki POS websites, apps, online stores, APIs, and support channels.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect business account details such as business name, owner name, email address, phone number, country, subscription status, device identifiers, branch information, and setup preferences.

When a business uses Piki POS, the app may store and sync business records such as products, services, categories, stock records, sales, receipts, invoices, customer credit records, public catalog orders, employee or user activity, payment references, and reports.

When customers place orders through a Piki online storefront, we may process customer-provided information such as name, phone number, delivery address, order notes, cart items, and order status.

When messaging features are enabled, we may process phone numbers, message content, message delivery metadata, and provider identifiers required to send WhatsApp or SMS messages.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and improve Piki POS, sync business data, operate online storefronts, process support requests, secure accounts, detect abuse, maintain audit logs, manage subscriptions, generate reports, and send messages requested by the business.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use a business's customer records to market unrelated products to that business's customers.

3. Business Customer Data

Businesses using Piki POS are responsible for the customer and transaction data they enter into the system. For this data, Piki POS acts as a service provider or processor that stores, syncs, and processes information on behalf of the business.

Businesses should only upload or sync data they are allowed to process and should inform their customers how their data is used for receipts, orders, delivery, payments, support, and business communication.

4. WhatsApp, SMS, and Third-Party Providers

If a business enables WhatsApp Business API or SMS messaging, Piki POS may share the necessary recipient, message, and delivery information with the configured messaging provider, such as Meta/WhatsApp or an SMS gateway, so the requested message can be sent.

For WhatsApp Business API, each business may connect and verify its own WhatsApp sender number through Meta. Piki POS stores the technical identifiers required to send messages for that business, such as WhatsApp Business Account ID and Phone Number ID.

5. Payments and Integrations

Piki POS may integrate with payment, tax, messaging, hosting, analytics, support, and infrastructure providers. We share only the information needed to provide the selected service, protect the platform, or meet legal and operational requirements.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. These include access controls, server-side credential storage, transport security where configured, audit logs, and separation of business data by business account.

No system is perfectly secure, so businesses should protect their devices, passwords, staff accounts, and API credentials.

7. Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide Piki POS, comply with legal obligations, maintain records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and support backups or recovery. A business may request account deletion or data export by contacting us.

8. Your Choices

Businesses can update account information, messaging settings, online store settings, and cloud sync preferences inside Piki POS where available. Customers who placed an order through a Piki storefront should contact the business they ordered from, or contact Piki POS support if they cannot reach the business.

9. Children's Privacy

Piki POS is designed for businesses and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Piki POS changes. If changes are material, we will update the date above and may provide additional notice through the website, app, or support channels.

11. Contact Us

For privacy questions, data requests, or support, contact us at support@pikipos.com.
